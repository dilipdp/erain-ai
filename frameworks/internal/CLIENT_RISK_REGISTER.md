

# ERAIN AI — CLIENT RISK REGISTER (v1.0)

Purpose:
To proactively identify, score, and manage delivery, data, financial, reputational, and execution risks across all client engagements.

This document is mandatory for:
- All Pro Audit engagements
- All OpsMind™ retainers
- All Impact AI™ enterprise deployments

------------------------------------------------------------
1. RISK SCORING MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------

Each risk is scored on:

Impact (I): 1–5
1 = Minimal impact
5 = Severe financial / reputational damage

Probability (P): 1–5
1 = Unlikely
5 = Highly likely

Detectability (D): 1–5
1 = Easy to detect early
5 = Hard to detect until damage occurs

Risk Score = I × P × D

Risk Categories:
- 1–20 → Low (Monitor)
- 21–50 → Medium (Mitigation Plan Required)
- 51–125 → High (Immediate Action + Escalation)

------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE RISK CATEGORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

A. DATA RISKS
- Incomplete / manipulated data
- ERP access delays
- Manual Excel dependency
- No audit trail
- Data privacy breach exposure

B. EXECUTION RISKS
- No internal owner assigned
- Weak leadership sponsorship
- Cross-functional resistance
- No meeting cadence
- “Report only” mindset (no action discipline)

C. FINANCIAL RISKS
- Payment delays
- Scope creep without change order
- Misaligned ROI expectations
- Budget freeze mid-engagement

D. REPUTATIONAL RISKS
- Overpromising by client stakeholders
- Internal politics
- Blame shifting culture
- Public escalation risk

E. STRATEGIC RISKS
- Engagement without quantified baseline
- Skipping Free → Pro sequence
- OpsMind without audit foundation
- No executive sponsor

------------------------------------------------------------
4. ESCALATION RULES
------------------------------------------------------------

Escalate to Founder immediately if:
- Risk Score > 60
- Payment risk > 30 days
- Data integrity cannot be verified
- No executive sponsor exists
- Engagement deviates from audit-first model

------------------------------------------------------------
5. CLIENT READINESS CHECK (PRE-DATA COLLECTION)
------------------------------------------------------------

Before collecting any documents:

✔ Executive sponsor identified  
✔ Data owner identified  
✔ Engagement scope signed  
✔ Baseline timeline agreed  
✔ Data privacy protocol signed  
✔ Payment terms defined  
✔ ROI definition agreed

------------------------------------------------------------
