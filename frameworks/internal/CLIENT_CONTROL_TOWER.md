## 3. Engagement Stages (Standardized)

All clients must move through these exact stages:

### Stage 0 – Discussion
- No documents collected
- High-level business understanding
- Industry framework mapping prepared internally

### Stage 1 – Readiness Confirmed
- Universal Execution Core configured
- Industry framework customized
- Data Intake Checklist prepared
- NDA template ready

### Stage 2 – Controlled Data Intake
- Documents requested in structured batches
- Data cataloged
- No conclusions shared prematurely

### Stage 3 – Free Audit AI™
- Leakage heatmap
- Ranked findings
- 30/60/90 roadmap
- Executive summary PDF

### Stage 4 – Pro Audit
- Quantified ₹ impact
- Root cause maps
- Execution blueprint
- Owner mapping

### Stage 5 – OpsMind™ Deployment
- KPI tracking
- Weekly review cadence
- Execution tracker live
- Risk monitoring active

---

## 4. Data Collection Discipline Rule

We DO NOT collect data unless:

- Industry framework is finalized
- KPI mapping exists
- Leakage categories are defined
- Risk register template is prepared
- Secure storage structure exists

If not ready → Do not request documents.

---

## 5. Weekly Internal Control Ritual (Non-Negotiable)

Every week:

1. Update client table
2. Review risk levels
3. Confirm next milestone for each client
4. Identify blockers
5. Decide: Pause / Advance / Clarify

No silent drift allowed.

---

## 6. Risk Level Definition

Low:
- Early discussion
- No commitments made

Medium:
- Formal discussion
- Expectations forming

High:
- Data shared but structure incomplete
- Risk of misalignment

Critical:
- Execution promised without framework
- Trust exposure

## 7. Large Client Protocol (MFG-02)

For the large manufacturing client:

- Risk register fully customized
- Engagement playbook rehearsed

No improvisation allowed.

## 8. Golden Rule

EraIn does not scale chaos.

We scale:
- Truth
- Structure
- Proof
- Discipline
- Measurable impact

If unsure → slow down.
If unclear → clarify.
If unprepared → do not collect data.
