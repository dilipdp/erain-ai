

# ERAIN AI — MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on the Effective Date by and between:

**EraIn AI**, including its affiliates and representatives ("EraIn"),  
and  
**[Client Legal Name]**, including its affiliates and representatives ("Client").

EraIn and Client may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore and/or engage in discussions relating to audit, operations intelligence, AI systems, consulting, implementation, or related services (the "Purpose").

In connection with this Purpose, each Party may disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to the other.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:

- Financial statements, cost structures, margin data
- Operational data, KPIs, production or logistics metrics
- Customer, vendor, pricing, and contract information
- Process documentation, internal workflows, SOPs
- Technical architecture, system designs, code, models
- Business plans, strategies, forecasts
- Any data shared via documents, spreadsheets, emails, meetings, dashboards, or systems
- Any analysis, notes, reports, or derivative materials created from such data

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:

- Is publicly available without breach of this Agreement
- Was lawfully known prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed without reference to disclosed information
- Is lawfully received from a third party without restriction

---

## 3. Obligations of Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

1. Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.
2. Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without written consent.
3. Restrict access to employees, contractors, or advisors strictly on a need-to-know basis.
4. Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information (and not less than reasonable care).
5. Not reverse-engineer, copy, or misuse proprietary methodologies or frameworks shared during engagement.

---

## 4. Data Protection & Security

- Confidential documents shall be transmitted via secure channels (encrypted email, secure drive, or approved systems).
- Both Parties shall implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards.
- If a data breach is suspected, the Receiving Party must notify the Disclosing Party promptly.

---

## 5. No License or Ownership Transfer

All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party.

Nothing in this Agreement grants any intellectual property rights, license, or ownership transfer unless explicitly agreed in writing.

EraIn retains ownership of its proprietary methodologies, frameworks, tools, AI systems, models, and operating processes.

---

## 6. Term

This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and remains in effect for three (3) years.

Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years from the date of disclosure, or longer if required by law.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction of Information

Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall:

- Return or securely destroy Confidential Information
- Confirm destruction in writing if requested

Archival copies retained for compliance purposes must remain confidential.

---

## 8. No Obligation to Proceed

This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further agreement or business relationship.

---

## 9. Remedies

Unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm.

The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and other legal remedies in addition to monetary damages.

---
