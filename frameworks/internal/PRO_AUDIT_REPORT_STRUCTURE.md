# 1. Executive Summary (2–3 Pages Max)

## 1.1 Snapshot

- Total Estimated Annual Leakage (₹ / $)
- Top 3 Value Leaks
- Recovery Potential (Conservative Estimate)
- 90-Day Focus Priorities
- Confidence Level (Low / Medium / High based on data quality)

## 1.2 What Leadership Must Know

- What is actually happening (vs reported)
- Where profit is silently leaking
- What must be fixed first
- What not to do right now

This section must be written for CXO-level clarity.  
No jargon. No technical noise.

# Non-Negotiable Quality Rules

1. No generic language.
2. Every claim must tie to data or observation.
3. No vague recommendations.
4. No slide bloat.
5. Max 30 pages unless enterprise complexity demands more.
6. Every action must have an owner.
