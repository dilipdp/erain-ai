# 3. CLIENT STRUCTURE MODEL

Each client engagement must have a defined structure.

## 3.1 Mandatory Roles

### 1. Engagement Lead (EL)
Accountable for:
- 30/60/90 plan ownership
- Weekly execution rhythm
- Escalation decisions
- Client confidence

Cannot:
- Accept scope changes without documentation
- Promise new deliverables without review

---

### 2. Execution Analyst (EA)
Responsible for:
- Leakage mapping
- KPI tracking
- Updating execution tracker weekly
- Preparing structured artifacts
- Maintaining version control

EA must never:
- Send informal insights without documented context
- Modify KPIs without approval

---

### 3. Founder Oversight (FO)
Role:
- Strategic validation
- Escalation handling
- Quality audit
- Major pivot approval

Founder does NOT:
- Run weekly execution
- Build trackers
- Fix client operational noise
- Attend routine delivery calls

Founder operates at architecture level only.

---

# 4. CAPACITY CONTROL SYSTEM

To maintain premium delivery quality:

- Maximum 4 heavy clients per Engagement Lead
- Maximum 6 concurrent clients per Execution Analyst
- Maximum 1 Red-risk client per EL at a time

If breached:
→ No new clients accepted.
→ Capacity rebalance required.
→ Governance review mandatory.

Growth without capacity discipline is forbidden.

---

# 5. STANDARD DELIVERY FLOW (NON-SKIPPABLE)

Every client must pass through:

Phase 1 — Structured Intake (No raw data yet)
Phase 2 — Audit Phase (Reality capture)
Phase 3 — Quantified Impact Mapping
Phase 4 — 90-Day Execution Blueprint
Phase 5 — Weekly Execution Cadence
Phase 6 — ROI Attribution & Review

No jumping to:
- Dashboards
- AI agents
- Automation
- Strategy decks

Without validated audit.

# 6. ESCALATION MATRIX (48-HOUR RULE)

## Green
- Minor blockers
- Slight KPI deviation
Handled by EA.

## Amber
- Ownership confusion
- Missed milestone
- Data integrity concerns
Handled by EL within 48 hours.

## Red
- Major execution breakdown
- Trust risk
- Data breach
- Leadership conflict
Founder notified within 24 hours.

No silent failures allowed.
