# 1️⃣ FREE AUDIT — OUTPUT STRUCTURE

## Deliverable Format
PDF (10–15 pages)  
Executive-ready, structured, branded  

## Structure

1. Cover Page
   - Client name
   - Industry
   - Audit date
   - Confidentiality statement

2. Executive Summary (1–2 pages)
   - Top 3 risk areas
   - Estimated impact range
   - Urgency classification
   - Immediate action signals

3. Ranked Findings
   - Issue
   - Estimated impact range
   - Severity score
   - Confidence level
   - Category (Leakage / Execution / Decision / Governance)

4. 30 / 60 / 90 Roadmap
   - What to fix
   - Owner role
   - Expected measurable shift
   - Checkpoint structure

5. Upgrade Path
   - Why deeper audit is required
   - What Pro Audit will quantify
   - Decision guidance
