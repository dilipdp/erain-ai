# 1. Purpose

The EraIn ROI Attribution Model™ defines how business impact is:

- Identified
- Quantified
- Linked to actions
- Assigned to owners
- Sustained over time
- Proven to leadership

This model ensures:

→ No vague “AI improved things”
→ No assumed impact
→ No dashboard vanity
→ No untraceable wins

Every claimed improvement must be:
1. Root-cause linked
2. Owner-driven
3. Time-bound
4. Financially attributable
5. Sustainably verified

---

# 2. Core Principle

Impact (Period) = (Baseline – Post-Change) × Volume × Time Window

Where:

- Baseline = Locked, time-stamped pre-intervention metric
- Post-Change = Measured, owner-validated metric after execution
- Volume = Verified operational scale (units / orders / hours / transactions)
- Time Window = Defined measurement period (days / weeks / months)

Additionally:

Confidence-Adjusted Impact = Impact × Confidence %

Where Confidence % is derived from:
- Data reliability (0–100%)
- Adoption completeness (0–100%)
- Measurement stability (0–100%)

No baseline → No ROI.  
No time window → No annualization.  
No owner → No execution.  
No confidence score → No executive reporting.

# 3. ROI Categories

All ROI is classified into one of 5 buckets:

## A. Cost Recovery
- Waste reduction
- Scrap reduction
- Rework reduction
- Vendor price correction
- Overtime reduction
- Energy optimization

Formula:
Recovered Cost = (Old Cost – New Cost) × Volume

---

## B. Revenue Protection
- Churn reduction
- SLA penalty avoidance
- Leakage prevention
- Claim recovery
- Missed billing correction

Formula:
Protected Revenue = Prevented Loss × Probability Adjustment

---

## C. Throughput Improvement
- Increased output
- Reduced downtime
- Faster cycle times
- Capacity unlock

Formula:
Throughput Gain = Additional Units × Contribution Margin

---

## D. Working Capital Release
- Inventory optimization
- Payment cycle improvement
- Procurement efficiency
- Dead stock liquidation

Formula:
Capital Released × Cost of Capital %

---

## E. Risk Avoidance
- Compliance penalty avoidance
- Safety incident prevention
- Audit failure prevention
- Regulatory exposure mitigation

Formula:
Expected Risk × Likelihood Reduction %

# 4. Attribution Integrity Levels

Every ROI claim is tagged:

Level 1 — Estimated
• Directionally modeled
• Not yet validated in real environment

Level 2 — Verified
• Baseline documented
• Post-change measured
• Owner-confirmed

Level 3 — Sustained
• Verified across ≥ 2 cycles
• Embedded in operating cadence

Level 4 — Institutionalized
• Documented SOP change
• Structural process redesign
• Governance-level adoption

Only Level 2+ can be presented as client ROI proof.

---

# 5. Baseline Discipline

Before execution:

1. Capture:
   - Metric name
   - Current value
   - Volume
   - Time period
   - Financial multiplier

2. Document:
   - Data source
   - Owner
   - Assumptions
   - Known distortions

3. Lock baseline snapshot.

No retroactive baselines allowed.

---

## 5A. Baseline Snapshot Protocol

Every baseline must include:

- Snapshot ID (unique reference)
- Timestamp (UTC)
- Data Source (ERP / Excel / Manual / System)
- Data Extraction Method
- Owner Sign-off (Name + Date)
- Assumptions Log
- Distortion Flags (seasonality, anomalies, one-time events)

Baseline snapshots are immutable.
Any correction creates a new versioned snapshot.

Retroactive baseline editing is strictly prohibited.

- Intervention Description

The ROI Ledger is the single source of financial truth.

# 7. Time Dimension

ROI is not one-time.

We measure:

- Immediate Impact (0–30 days)
- Stabilized Impact (30–90 days)
- Sustained Impact (90+ days)
- Annualized Impact

Annualized Impact = Monthly Impact × 12
Only after 90-day stability.

- No stacked improvement assumptions
- No unverified extrapolation
- No assumed full adoption without proof
- No compounding without time evidence

“What exactly changed?”
“Who made it change?”
“Where is it documented?”
