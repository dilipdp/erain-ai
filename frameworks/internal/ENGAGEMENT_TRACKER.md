## Stage Definitions

**DISCOVERY**  
Initial conversation. No data collected.

**QUALIFIED**  
Clear problem statement. Fit confirmed. Awaiting NDA.

**NDA SIGNED**  
Legal clearance complete. Data request template can be sent.

**DATA RECEIVED**  
Documents received but not yet processed.

**AUDIT RUNNING**  
Analysis in progress.

**PRO AUDIT READY**  
Deep analysis prepared. Review pending.

**EXECUTION LIVE**  
OpsMind or Impact AI active.

**PAUSED**  
Engagement temporarily halted.

**CLOSED**  
Completed or declined.

---

## Risk Classification

LOW → Small scope, limited operational complexity  
MEDIUM → Multi-department or moderate operational depth  
HIGH → Multi-site / financial exposure / compliance heavy  
CRITICAL → Enterprise-wide / public / regulatory risk  

---

## Engagement Control Rules

1. Maximum 2 active “Audit Running” engagements at a time.
2. No Pro Audit before Free Audit findings are documented.
3. No execution without written owner confirmation.
4. No data stored outside approved secure environment.
5. Every engagement must have:
   - Clear scope
   - Defined owner
   - Expected outcome metric
   - Defined timeline

---

## Weekly Control Ritual

Every week, review:

- Active audits
- Bottlenecks
- Risk exposure
- Founder time allocation
- Cash flow visibility

If overload risk detected:
→ Pause new intake.
