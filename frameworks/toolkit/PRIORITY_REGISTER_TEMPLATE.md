# PRIORITY REGISTER TEMPLATE  
## EraIn Execution Control System™

This document is mandatory for every EraIn client after Audit or Pro Audit.

It converts insights into controlled execution.

No priority enters execution without being logged here.

---

# 1️⃣ MASTER PRIORITY REGISTER

Each priority must follow this structure.

---

## Priority ID:
(Example: MFG-PR-001 / LOG-PR-004 / SOL-PR-002)

## Title:
Clear, specific problem statement.

## Category:
- Leakage  
- Throughput  
- Quality  
- Cost  
- Delay  
- Risk  
- Governance  
- Revenue  

## Source:
- Audit Finding  
- Pro Audit Deep Dive  
- Leadership Escalation  
- KPI Alert  
- Incident  

## Problem Statement:
What exactly is happening?  
Be factual. No emotional wording.

## Verified Root Cause:
Root cause — not symptom.  
(Attach root-cause map reference if applicable.)

## Financial Impact:
- Current Loss (₹ / $ per month)
- Potential Recovery
- ROI Estimate (%)

## Operational Impact:
- Teams affected
- Sites affected
- Process stage impacted

---

# 2️⃣ EXECUTION DESIGN

## Priority Level:
- P1 – Critical (Immediate revenue/risk impact)
- P2 – High (Material performance improvement)
- P3 – Medium (Efficiency optimization)
- P4 – Low (Long-term refinement)

## Owner:
Single accountable owner (no shared ownership)

## Supporting Team:
Execution contributors

## Target Outcome:
What must be measurably different in 30–60 days?

## KPI Linkage:
Which KPI(s) will reflect improvement?

## Baseline Metric:
Current verified number

## Target Metric:
Expected outcome number

---

# 3️⃣ EXECUTION CONTROL

## Start Date:
## Target Completion Date:
## Cadence:
- Weekly review
- Bi-weekly review
- Monthly review

## Checkpoints:
List milestone checkpoints.

---

# 4️⃣ RISK CONTROL

## Execution Risks:
What could block delivery?

## Mitigation Plan:
How do we prevent slippage?

---

# 5️⃣ PROOF & CLOSURE

## Evidence of Completion:
What measurable proof confirms closure?

## Financial Recovery Verified:
₹ / $ impact validated

## Governance Sign-Off:
- Owner Signature
- Leadership Confirmation
- EraIn Validation

---

# RULES

1. No execution without an Owner.
2. No closure without proof.
3. No priority without financial or operational impact.
4. No vague language.
5. No “in progress” for more than 30 days without escalation.
