

# EraIn Audit Scoring Engine™  
**Version 1.0 — Universal Execution Core Compatible**

---

## 1. Purpose

The Audit Scoring Engine converts qualitative findings into:

- Numeric Severity Scores  
- Financial Impact Estimates  
- Priority Ranking  
- Execution Order  

It ensures that every audit output is:

- Quantified  
- Comparable  
- Repeatable  
- CFO-defensible  

No vague insights. Only measurable impact.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy

Every issue is scored across five universal dimensions:

1. Financial Impact  
2. Frequency  
3. Controllability  
4. Strategic Risk  
5. Execution Complexity  

Final output:

> **Priority Score (0–100)**  
> + Estimated Annual Financial Leakage (₹ / $)

---

## 3. Scoring Model (Universal Formula)

### 3.1 Dimension Weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Financial Impact | 30% |
| Frequency | 20% |
| Strategic Risk | 20% |
| Controllability | 15% |
| Execution Complexity (inverse) | 15% |

Total = 100%

---

## 4. Dimension Definitions

### 4.1 Financial Impact (0–5)

Score based on annual estimated leakage.

| Score | Annual Impact |
|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Minor (<1% revenue impact) |
| 2 | Small but noticeable |
| 3 | Moderate impact |
| 4 | High impact |
| 5 | Severe / multi-layer loss |

---

### 4.2 Frequency (0–5)

How often does this issue occur?

| Score | Frequency |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Occasional |
| 3 | Monthly |
| 4 | Weekly |
| 5 | Daily / Continuous |

---

### 4.3 Strategic Risk (0–5)

Impact on long-term positioning.

| Score | Risk Level |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Local issue |
| 2 | Departmental |
| 3 | Multi-function |
| 4 | Enterprise-level |
| 5 | Existential / regulatory |

---

### 4.4 Controllability (0–5)

How much control does leadership have?

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Hard to influence |
| 3 | Partial control |
| 5 | Fully controllable internally |

Higher controllability = higher priority.

---

### 4.5 Execution Complexity (0–5)

Effort required to fix.

| Score | Complexity |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Easy fix |
| 3 | Medium effort |
| 5 | Major transformation |

⚠ This dimension is inversely weighted (harder fixes slightly reduce priority score).

---

## 5. Final Priority Formula

```
Priority Score =
(Financial × 0.30)
+ (Frequency × 0.20)
+ (Strategic × 0.20)
+ (Controllability × 0.15)
+ ((5 - Complexity) × 0.15)
```

Multiply final result by 20 → convert to 0–100 scale.

---

## 6. Financial Leakage Estimation Model

### 6.1 Direct Loss Formula

```
Leakage = 
(Unit Loss × Volume × Frequency)
+ (Downtime Cost)
+ (Penalty / Rework / Wastage)
```

### 6.2 Example (Manufacturing)

- Scrap: ₹300/unit  
- Volume: 2,000 units/month  
- Annual impact:

₹300 × 2,000 × 12 = ₹72,00,000/year
