# 1. PURPOSE

The Industry Benchmark Library exists to answer one question:

**“Compared to what good looks like — where do we stand?”**

This library enables:

• Context-aware performance evaluation  
• Industry-adjusted leakage detection  
• Severity grading (Low / Medium / High / Critical)  
• Priority scoring based on deviation from benchmark  
• Reality-based ROI estimation  

Without benchmarks, audits are opinions.  
With benchmarks, audits become proof.

---

# 2. DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Benchmarks are:

• Directional, not absolute  
• Tier-adjusted (Small / Mid / Large enterprise)  
• Region-sensitive (India vs Global context where relevant)  
• Conservative by default (no inflated promises)  
• Updated periodically  

Benchmarks are used to:

1. Identify abnormal performance
2. Grade severity
3. Estimate recovery potential
4. Support executive discussions

# 4. UNIVERSAL CROSS-INDUSTRY BENCHMARKS

These apply across almost every industry.

---

## 4.1 Revenue Leakage %

Healthy: < 3%  
Warning: 3–7%  
Critical: > 7%  

Interpretation:
Above 7% indicates systemic process breakdown or pricing leakage.

Recovery Potential:
Up to 60–80% of leakage is typically recoverable within 6–12 months.

---

## 4.2 Decision Latency (Days)

Healthy: < 3 days  
Warning: 3–7 days  
Critical: > 7 days  

Interpretation:
High latency indicates unclear ownership or escalation failure.

Recovery Potential:
Faster decisions reduce secondary leakage by 10–25%.

---

## 4.3 Rework Rate %

Healthy: < 5%  
Warning: 5–12%  
Critical: > 12%  

Interpretation:
Above 12% signals training gaps or process design failure.

Recovery Potential:
Reducible by 30–50% through structured execution discipline.

---

## 4.4 Unowned Tasks %

Healthy: < 2%  
Warning: 2–6%  
Critical: > 6%  

Interpretation:
Tasks without clear owner lead to cascading delays.

Recovery Potential:
Ownership clarity alone reduces delays by 20–35%.

# 9. SEVERITY SCORING MODEL

Each KPI deviation is scored:

Green = 0  
Yellow = 1  
Orange = 2  
Red = 3  

Total Severity Score =
Sum of weighted KPI deviations

Priority Ranking:
0–3 → Stable  
4–8 → Moderate Risk  
9–15 → High Risk  
16+ → Critical Intervention Required
