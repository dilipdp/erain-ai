

# ERAIN AI — AUDIT SCORING TEMPLATE  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Standardized scoring model for all EraIn Audit AI™ and Pro Audit™ engagements  
**Scope:** Industry-agnostic (Manufacturing, Services, Logistics, Solar, Retail, etc.)

---

# 1. Scoring Philosophy

EraIn does not score based on “how bad things look.”  
We score based on **Impact × Control × Urgency × Evidence Strength**.

Every audit finding must:

- Be observable
- Be measurable
- Be attributable
- Have an accountable owner
- Tie to financial or operational impact

---

# 2. Core Scoring Formula

For every finding:

Final Score = (Impact × Control Gap × Urgency × Evidence Strength)

Each variable scored 1–5.

Maximum Score = 625  
Minimum Score = 1  

---

# 3. Scoring Variables

## 3.1 Impact (1–5)

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Minor inefficiency, no measurable financial effect |
| 2 | Small recurring cost or delay |
| 3 | Noticeable margin / time loss |
| 4 | Significant operational or financial damage |
| 5 | Strategic-level threat to profit, compliance, or continuity |

---

## 3.2 Control Gap (1–5)

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Fully monitored and controlled |
| 2 | Basic tracking exists |
| 3 | Partial visibility, inconsistent ownership |
| 4 | No structured control |
| 5 | Blind spot — no measurement, no owner |

---

## 3.3 Urgency (1–5)

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Can wait 6+ months |
| 2 | Improvement opportunity |
| 3 | Should address within quarter |
| 4 | Immediate margin or performance risk |
| 5 | Active damage occurring now |

---

## 3.4 Evidence Strength (1–5)

| Score | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Hypothesis only |
| 2 | Anecdotal confirmation |
| 3 | Some data validation |
| 4 | Clear documented evidence |
| 5 | Fully verified with quantified proof |

---

# 4. Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Priority Level | Action |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| 1–50 | Low | Monitor |
| 51–150 | Moderate | Improve within 90 days |
| 151–300 | High | 30–60 day action |
| 301–450 | Critical | Immediate intervention |
| 451–625 | Severe | Executive escalation |

---

# 5. Financial Mapping Layer

After scoring, map each finding to:

- Estimated ₹/$ Loss per Month
- Recovery Potential %
- Time-to-Recovery
- Required Owner
- Required Cadence (Weekly / Biweekly / Monthly)

Example:

| Finding | Score | Monthly Loss | Recovery % | Owner | Cadence |
|----------|-------|--------------|------------|--------|---------|
| Maintenance Downtime | 412 | ₹8,50,000 | 40% | Plant Head | Weekly |

---

# 6. Audit Categories (Universal)

Every audit must score across:

1. Revenue Leakage
2. Cost Leakage
3. Process Bottlenecks
4. Ownership & Accountability
5. Decision Latency
6. Vendor / Supply Risk
7. Asset Utilization
8. Compliance / Governance
9. Customer Retention / SLA Risk
10. Execution Cadence

Each category must have:

- At least 1 quantified finding
- At least 1 owner
- At least 1 time-bound action

---

# 7. Output Structure (Client-Ready)

Audit output must include:

1. Top 5 Findings (Ranked by Score)
2. Financial Exposure Summary
3. 30 / 60 / 90 Day Action Plan
4. Owner Assignment
5. ROI Projection
6. Confidence Level (based on evidence strength average)

---

# 8. Audit Confidence Index

Confidence Index = Average Evidence Strength Score

| Score | Confidence Level |
|--------|------------------|
| 1–2 | Exploratory |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | High |
| 5 | Executive-Grade |

---

# 9. Audit Maturity Benchmark

We also calculate:

Operational Maturity Score =  
(Avg Control Score + Avg Ownership Score + Avg Cadence Score) ÷ 3

This positions client in one of:

- Reactive
- Controlled
- Structured
- Optimized
- Scalable

---

# 10. Mandatory Audit Rules

- No scoring without documented evidence
- No recommendation without owner
- No impact claim without financial mapping
- No rollout without cadence
- No “AI experiment” without ROI attribution

---
