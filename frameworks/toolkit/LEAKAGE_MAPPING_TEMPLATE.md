# PURPOSE

This template is the core diagnostic instrument of EraIn Audit AI™ and Pro Audit™.
It is designed to:

• Detect hidden value leakage
• Quantify financial impact conservatively
• Identify root causes (not symptoms)
• Assign accountable ownership
• Design execution recovery paths
• Enable provable ROI attribution

This framework is industry-agnostic.
It applies to manufacturing, services, logistics, retail, infra, solar, healthcare, SaaS, public sector, and any operational organization.

No assumptions.
No theory.
Only mapped, evidenced, quantified leakage.

# 2. UNIVERSAL LEAKAGE CLASSIFICATION

All leakages MUST be classified into one primary category.

A. Financial Leakages
- Margin erosion
- Cost overruns
- Idle capital
- Pricing leakage
- Vendor overpayment
- Discount misuse
- Claims / warranty / rework cost
- Revenue leakage

B. Operational Leakages
- Downtime
- Delay
- Rework
- Underutilization
- SLA miss
- Bottlenecks
- Inventory mismatch
- Poor scheduling

C. Decision Leakages
- Delayed approvals
- Escalation loops
- Decision latency
- Lack of owner
- Conflicting priorities
- No data-backed prioritization

D. Compliance & Risk Leakages
- Audit failures
- Untracked exceptions
- Safety gaps
- CAPA delays
- Regulatory exposure
- Control breakdown

E. Human & Structural Leakages
- Role ambiguity
- Shift discipline failure
- Multi-site inconsistency
- Poor handoffs
- Training gaps
- Incentive misalignment

# 5. PRIORITIZATION MODEL

Each leakage must be scored 1–5 in each dimension.

Impact (Financial magnitude)
Frequency (How often it occurs)
Ease of Fix (Inverse complexity)
Strategic Weight (Leadership alignment)

Priority Score = Impact × Frequency × Ease × Strategic Weight

Add final ranking column.

# 9. DATA REQUEST CHECKLIST (BEFORE COLLECTION)

Do NOT collect data until ready.

When ready, request only relevant:

- Revenue & margin reports (last 6–12 months)
- Cost breakdown by function
- Downtime logs / SLA reports
- Vendor contracts / rate sheets
- Inventory aging reports
- Escalation or incident logs
- Organizational structure chart

Request in controlled batches.
Never request everything blindly.
