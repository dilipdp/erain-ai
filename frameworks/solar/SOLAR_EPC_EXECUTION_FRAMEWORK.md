# 2. Universal Solar Failure Modes (Reality Layer)

Across 90% of EPC businesses, these are the silent killers:

- Under-quoted BOS & electrical infra
- Transformer / HT approval delays
- Site idle labor & material blocking
- Vendor mismatch (structure vs module vs inverter timelines)
- Poor commissioning documentation
- CEIG / DISCOM compliance delays
- No cost-to-serve tracking per kW
- O&M reactive instead of predictive
- No real ROI proof after plant handover

EraIn installs controls to eliminate these.
