# 3. Stage-by-Stage Execution Framework

## STAGE 1 — Context & Structure Mapping

Objective:
Understand how the organization actually operates.

Collect:
- Org structure
- Roles & owners
- Process maps (if exist)
- KPIs currently tracked
- Reporting cadence
- Decision hierarchy

Output:
- Operational topology map
- Ownership clarity map
- KPI reality check

Red Flags:
- No clear owner
- Reports without actions
- KPI not tied to accountability
- Delayed feedback loops

---

## STAGE 2 — Value Flow Mapping

Objective:
Trace how money/time/value flows.

Map:
- Revenue generation flow
- Cost structure
- Procurement flow
- Production/service delivery flow
- Customer lifecycle
- Escalation chain

Output:
- Value flow diagram
- Delay points
- Rework loops
- Approval bottlenecks

---

## STAGE 3 — Leak Detection Engine

We classify leaks into 6 universal categories:

1. Time Leakage
2. Cost Leakage
3. Capacity Leakage
4. Quality Leakage
5. Decision Leakage
6. Accountability Leakage

For each leak we record:

- Location
- Owner
- Evidence
- Estimated impact
- Frequency
- Fix complexity

This becomes the Leak Register™.

---

## STAGE 4 — Root Cause Mapping

We NEVER stop at surface symptom.

Use:
- 5 Whys
- Owner gap analysis
- Incentive misalignment
- Process bottleneck mapping
- Control failure review

Output:
Root Cause Map for Top 3–5 leaks

This separates EraIn from typical consulting.

---

## STAGE 5 — Impact Quantification Model

We calculate:

Annual Impact = (Leak Value × Frequency × Scale Multiplier)

Examples:
- Downtime hours × hourly loss × months
- Margin erosion × volume × cycle frequency
- Delay penalty × projects per year

We classify impact:

Tier A — > 5% EBITDA impact  
Tier B — 1–5% impact  
Tier C — < 1% impact  

Only Tier A & B enter 90-day plan.
