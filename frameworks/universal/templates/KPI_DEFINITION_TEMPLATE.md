# KPI Governance Rules

1. No vanity KPIs.
2. Every KPI must map to financial or strategic impact.
3. Every KPI must have a decision owner.
4. Every KPI must have escalation logic.
5. Every KPI must be measurable with auditable data.
6. Every KPI must support ROI attribution.

If a KPI violates any of the above, it must be redesigned or deleted.
