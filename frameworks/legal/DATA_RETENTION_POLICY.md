# ERAIN AI – DATA RETENTION & DELETION POLICY
Version: 1.0  
Classification: Legal – Governance Critical  
Owner: Founder  
Effective Date: ____________________

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This policy defines how EraIn AI retains client data, derived artifacts, reports, and engagement records, and how such data is securely deleted or archived.

Retention discipline protects:

• Client confidentiality  
• Legal defensibility  
• Regulatory compliance  
• Brand trust  

EraIn retains only what is necessary. Nothing more.

---

# 2. DATA CATEGORIES

Data is grouped into the following categories:

A. Raw Client Data  
B. Processed / Modeled Data  
C. Audit Outputs & Findings  
D. Final Reports & Executive Decks  
E. Engagement Governance Records  
F. Financial & Contractual Documents  

Each category has defined retention rules.

---

# 3. RETENTION PERIODS

## A. Raw Client Data
Retention: 12 months after engagement closure  
Purpose: Audit defense, recalculation, dispute resolution  
After 12 months:
• Deleted securely OR  
• Archived if legally required  

## B. Processed / Modeled Data
Retention: 24 months  
Condition:
• Must not contain identifiable sensitive data if retained  
• Must be anonymized for internal learning reuse  

## C. Audit Outputs & Findings
Retention: 3 years  
Purpose:
• Proof of delivery  
• Legal protection  
• ROI attribution validation  

## D. Final Reports & Executive Decks
Retention: 3 years minimum  

## E. Governance & Risk Registers
Retention: 3 years  

## F. Financial & Contractual Documents
Retention: As required by governing law (typically 5–7 years)

---

# 4. EARLY DELETION RIGHTS

Client may request deletion of raw data at any time after project completion.

Upon request:

1. Confirm scope of deletion  
2. Delete raw data  
3. Confirm deletion in writing  
4. Retain only legally required documents  

Deletion confirmation must be documented.

---

# 5. SECURE DELETION STANDARDS

Deletion must:

• Remove files from primary storage  
• Clear backup storage where applicable  
• Not leave recoverable traces in shared folders  
• Be logged in deletion register  

No casual deletion.

All deletions are recorded.

---

# 6. ARCHIVAL POLICY

Archived data must:

• Be encrypted  
• Be access-restricted  
• Be stored in designated archive directory  
• Be labeled with retention expiry date  

Archive structure example:

/archive/
   /Client_Name/
       /retention_expiry_YYYY/

---

# 7. RETENTION REVIEW CYCLE

Every quarter:

✔ Review expiring retention records  
✔ Delete eligible raw data  
✔ Update deletion log  
✔ Confirm compliance  

Founder must review deletion register annually.

---

# 8. BREACH OF RETENTION POLICY

Failure to delete eligible data may result in:

• Internal compliance escalation  
• Legal exposure  
• Client trust damage  

Retention discipline is mandatory.
