# 5. LPI (LEAKAGE PRESSURE INDEX)

Each category scored 1–5:

1 = Stable
3 = Moderate pressure
5 = Severe leakage

Categories:
- Revenue leakage
- Effort leakage
- Process instability
- Governance weakness

Composite Score:
- 0–5 Low
- 6–10 Moderate
- 11–15 High
- 16–20 Critical
