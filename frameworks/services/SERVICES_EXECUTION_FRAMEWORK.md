## 2. Why Services Fail (Common Patterns)

Across service companies, failure modes are consistent:

1. Revenue leakage (underbilling, scope creep)
2. Low utilization
3. Rework loops
4. Weak project ownership
5. Delivery delays
6. Poor client communication cadence
7. No margin visibility per project
8. No post-mortem discipline
9. Talent mismatch vs task
10. Decision latency

EraIn does not install dashboards first.  
EraIn installs execution loops first.

---

### Universal Leakage Classification (Aligned to Universal Execution Core™)

All service failures are mapped into 7 Universal Leakage Types:

1. Revenue Leakage (underbilling, scope creep, discount drift)
2. Cost Leakage (rework, overtime, shadow non-billable effort)
3. Time Leakage (delivery delays, approval latency)
4. Capacity Leakage (misallocation, low utilization, idle load)
5. Decision Leakage (unclear ownership, escalation gaps)
6. Trust Leakage (client dissatisfaction, SLA breaches)
7. Structural Leakage (pricing model flaws, weak contract design)

Every audit finding must be tagged to at least one of these categories.
