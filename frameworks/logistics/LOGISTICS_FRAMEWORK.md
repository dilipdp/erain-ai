# 3. LOGISTICS LEAKAGE LIBRARY™

## 3.1 Financial Leakages

- Route inefficiency (fuel overuse)
- Idle fleet time
- Low load factor
- Detention penalties
- Late delivery penalties
- Inventory shrinkage
- Warehouse space under-utilization
- Manual reconciliation losses
- Claim settlement delays
- Vendor overbilling

## 3.2 Operational Leakages

- Delay variance vs planned ETA
- Repeated route deviations
- Dispatch approval bottlenecks
- Handoff failure between warehouse & fleet
- Inconsistent dispatch prioritization
- SLA breach frequency
- Low fleet utilization %
- Excess turnaround time (TAT)
- Poor cross-docking coordination

## 3.3 Governance Leakages

- No owner for delay root cause
- No incident RCA tracking
- No penalty recovery tracking
- Weak vendor SLA enforcement
- Missing audit trail for approvals
- No weekly performance cadence
