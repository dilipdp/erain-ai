1. Industry Sample Executive Summary (relevant sector)  
2. 90-Day Execution Blueprint (high-level)  
3. Governance installation philosophy

“We do not begin with data collection. We begin by aligning on execution discipline, ownership clarity, and measurable recovery targets.”
