## How to Use This Dashboard

1. Every client engagement must instantiate this template.
2. Free Audit populates Sections 1 and 4.
3. Pro Audit completes Financial Impact + Root Cause depth.
4. OpsMind activates Weekly Cadence + KPI Monitoring.
5. Impact AI activates ROI Attribution + Governance tracking.
6. No initiative is considered complete unless:
   - Owner assigned
   - Financial impact estimated
   - Measured outcome verified
   - Sustainability risk reviewed

---

**Principle:**  
EraIn does not track activity.  
EraIn tracks impact.
