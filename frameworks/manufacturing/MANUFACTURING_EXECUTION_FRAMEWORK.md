# 3. MANUFACTURING FAILURE MODES (UNIVERSAL)

## 3.0 Universal Value Leakage Categories (EraIn Core)

All manufacturing issues must first be mapped to one of the 7 Universal Leakage Types:

1. Throughput Leakage  
2. Cost Leakage  
3. Asset Utilization Leakage  
4. Labor Productivity Leakage  
5. Quality Leakage  
6. Working Capital Leakage  
7. Governance & Decision Leakage  

Only after classification under these categories do we diagnose plant-specific symptoms.

Across all manufacturing environments, losses typically occur in:

- Downtime leakage
- Poor OEE visibility
- Rework and scrap
- Maintenance misalignment
- Procurement inefficiency
- Vendor unreliability
- Idle labor
- Capacity bottlenecks
- Poor shift handover discipline
- No ownership clarity
- Decision latency at plant level
- Cost not tied to operational drivers

## 3.1 Impact Classification Model

Each identified issue is classified under:

Tier 1 — EBITDA Critical  
Direct financial leakage measurable within 90 days.

Tier 2 — Structural Risk  
Medium-term profitability erosion.

Tier 3 — Governance Weakness  
Does not immediately show in P&L but increases systemic risk.

This ensures prioritization is financial, not emotional.

Every identified issue must therefore:
- Map to one Universal Leakage Type
- Be quantified in ₹ / $
- Have a named accountable owner
- Be assigned a decision deadline
- Be tracked to financial proof

This ensures strict compliance with the Universal Execution Core™.
