# 8 — Financial Quantification Engine (Hard ROI Logic)

Manufacturing audits must convert operational gaps into defensible financial impact.

## A. Downtime Loss Formula

Downtime Impact (₹) =
(Hourly Contribution Margin) × (Unplanned Downtime Hours)

Where:
Hourly Contribution Margin =
(Revenue per Hour − Variable Cost per Hour)

---

## B. Scrap Impact Formula

Scrap Impact (₹) =
(Scrap % − Benchmark Scrap %) × Total Production Volume × Contribution per Unit

---

## C. OEE Improvement Value

OEE Gain Value =
(OEE Gap %) × Installed Capacity × Contribution per Unit

---

## D. Working Capital Release

Working Capital Impact =
(Inventory Days − Benchmark Days) × Average Daily COGS

---

## E. Maintenance Reactivity Cost

Reactive Premium Cost =
(Reactive Ratio − Benchmark Ratio) × Maintenance Budget

All formulas must be:
- Transparent
- Documented
- Included in Appendix
- Reproducible

No “AI estimated savings” allowed.
