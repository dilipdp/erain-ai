# MANUFACTURING EXECUTION GOVERNANCE MODEL
Version: 1.0  
Purpose: Define how EraIn installs execution discipline inside Manufacturing clients  
Classification: Internal – Operational Control Critical  

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Diagnosis does NOT create impact.  
Execution discipline creates impact.

EraIn does not stop at analysis.  
EraIn installs cadence, ownership, and accountability.

If governance is weak → ROI collapses.

---

# 1 — EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

Manufacturing Governance has 5 layers:

1. KPI Definition Layer
2. Owner Accountability Layer
3. Weekly Cadence Layer
4. Escalation Layer
5. Executive Review Layer

All 5 must operate simultaneously.

---

# 2 — KPI DEFINITION LAYER

Each engagement must define:

• 3–5 Primary KPIs (Plant-level)
• 5–10 Secondary KPIs (Line-level)
• Clear formula definitions
• Data source validation

Examples:

Primary:
• OEE
• Throughput (Units/Hour)
• Scrap %
• Downtime %

Secondary:
• MTBF
• MTTR
• Changeover Time
• Rework %

KPI Rule:
If it cannot be measured weekly → it is not a governance KPI.

---

# 3 — OWNER ACCOUNTABILITY MODEL

Every action must have:

• Single accountable owner (not committee)
• Defined KPI linkage
• Review frequency
• Escalation contact

Ownership Mapping Table:

| KPI | Owner | Backup | Review Cadence | Escalation Level |

Never assign shared ownership for critical KPIs.

Accountability must be explicit.

---

# 4 — WEEKLY CADENCE STRUCTURE

Weekly Manufacturing Review Meeting:

Duration: 60–90 minutes  
Participants: Plant Head, Production, Maintenance, Quality, EraIn  

Agenda Structure:

1. KPI Snapshot (15 min)
   • OEE trend
   • Downtime trend
   • Scrap trend
   • Throughput vs target

2. Variance Review (20 min)
   • Top deviations
   • Root cause
   • Immediate containment

3. Action Review (20 min)
   • Open actions
   • Delays
   • Owner accountability

4. Escalation Items (15 min)
   • Cross-functional blockers
   • Capex needs
   • Policy barriers

5. Next Week Commitments (10 min)

Meeting Rule:
No discussion without data.

---

# 5 — ESCALATION LADDER

Level 1 — Line Supervisor  
Level 2 — Plant Head  
Level 3 — Operations Director / CXO  

Escalation Trigger:

• KPI deviation > agreed threshold
• Action delay > 2 weeks
• Recurring failure pattern

Escalation must be time-bound.

No silent backlog.

---

# 6 — DECISION LOG STRUCTURE

Every material decision must be recorded:

| Date | Decision | Owner | Impact | Risk | Review Date |

Decision transparency prevents:
• Repetition
• Blame shifting
• Loss of institutional memory

---

# 7 — 30/60/90 EXECUTION INSTALLATION

Phase 1 (0–30 Days)
• Install KPI dashboard
• Assign owners
• Begin weekly cadence
• Capture baseline

Phase 2 (30–60 Days)
• Eliminate top 2 leakage drivers
• Reduce downtime or scrap measurably
• Validate early ROI

Phase 3 (60–90 Days)
• Stabilize improvement
• Institutionalize monitoring
• Build internal review rhythm

Governance must outlive EraIn involvement.

---

# 8 — RISK GOVERNANCE

Each plant must maintain:

• Risk register
• Failure mode mapping
• Mitigation owner
• Review cadence

Risk categories:
• Operational
• Maintenance
• Demand
• Workforce
• Compliance

---

# 9 — METRIC INTEGRITY RULE

KPI Manipulation Risk:

If pressure increases, metrics get distorted.

Protection:

• Data source locking
• Cross-verification
• Random sampling
• Transparent formula definitions

Trust collapses if metrics are manipulated.

---

# 10 — EXECUTIVE REVIEW STRUCTURE (MONTHLY)

Monthly CXO Review:

• OEE trend vs baseline
• Financial recovery validation
• Bottleneck shift analysis
• Risk exposure review
• ROI validation status

Must include:

• Conservative impact number
• Stress-case exposure

---

# 11 — GOVERNANCE MATURITY SCALE

Level 1 — Reactive  
Level 2 — Measured  
Level 3 — Managed  
Level 4 — Controlled  
Level 5 — Institutionalized  

EraIn target:
Bring client to Level 4 before disengagement.

---

# 12 — EXIT CRITERIA

EraIn engagement considered execution-complete when:

[ ] KPIs tracked without EraIn prompting  
[ ] Weekly cadence self-sustained  
[ ] Owners escalate proactively  
[ ] Financial impact validated 2 consecutive months  
[ ] Governance documentation internalized  

Only then consider scaling or case study.

---

# DISCIPLINE RULE

Do not confuse:

• Activity with execution
• Meetings with accountability
• Dashboards with discipline

Execution is measured in sustained KPI improvement.
