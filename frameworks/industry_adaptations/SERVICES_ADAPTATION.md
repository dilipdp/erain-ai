# 1. HOW SERVICES BUSINESSES LEAK VALUE

Unlike manufacturing (asset-heavy), services are:
- People-heavy
- Time-based
- Utilization-driven
- SLA-sensitive
- Margin-fragile

Most service firms don’t fail from lack of sales.
They fail from **execution leakage**.

### Primary Service Leak Categories

## 1.1 Utilization Leakage
- Low billable utilization
- Bench time not visible
- Senior staff doing junior work
- Idle time between projects
- Over-hiring before pipeline converts

Impact:
→ Margin erosion
→ Hidden payroll inefficiency
→ Profitability illusion

---

## 1.2 Scope Creep & Uncontrolled Effort
- Extra work not billed
- Change requests not tracked
- Underestimated project effort
- Poor requirements capture

Impact:
→ Projects look “successful”
→ But actual margin collapses

---

## 1.3 Delivery Delays
- Weak project cadence
- No milestone visibility
- Handoff failures
- Poor cross-team coordination

Impact:
→ SLA penalties
→ Reputation risk
→ Client churn

---

## 1.4 Pricing & Cost-to-Serve Blindness
- No project-level P&L
- No client profitability tracking
- Uniform pricing across unequal complexity
- Underpriced long-term accounts

Impact:
→ Revenue grows
→ Profit doesn’t

---

## 1.5 Sales-to-Delivery Disconnect
- Sales over-promises
- Delivery inherits unrealistic scope
- No feedback loop

Impact:
→ Delivery stress
→ Client dissatisfaction
→ Burnout
