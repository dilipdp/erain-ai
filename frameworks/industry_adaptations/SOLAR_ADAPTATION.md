# 1. Why Solar Needs a Specialized Layer

Solar businesses combine:

- Project execution (EPC)
- Engineering accuracy
- Field workforce coordination
- Vendor & procurement discipline
- Regulatory & DISCOM approvals
- Long-term asset performance (O&M)
- Capex-heavy cash flow structures

Failure in any layer creates:

• Margin leakage  
• Schedule slippage  
• Working capital stress  
• Underperforming plants  
• Reputation damage  

Solar is NOT just manufacturing.  
It is a **project + asset + compliance + performance business**.

# 2. Solar Failure Modes (Industry Reality)

## 2.1 Pre-Sales Leakage
- Wrong system sizing
- Unrealistic generation assumptions
- Poor shadow analysis
- Underestimated BOS costs
- Underpriced EPC quotes

## 2.2 Procurement Leakage
- Module price volatility mismanaged
- Inverter misalignment with load profile
- Late vendor locking
- Over-ordering / under-ordering
- Logistics penalties

## 2.3 Execution Leakage (Site Level)
- Delay in mounting structure
- Poor cable routing planning
- Rework due to design mismatch
- Idle labor due to material delays
- Unsafe practices → stoppages

## 2.4 Commissioning & Compliance
- DISCOM approval delays
- Net meter coordination failure
- CEIG compliance gaps
- Incomplete documentation packages

## 2.5 O&M Leakage
- Low PR (Performance Ratio)
- Module soiling not tracked
- Inverter faults ignored
- Generation underperformance vs proposal
- Poor ticket closure cycle
