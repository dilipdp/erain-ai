---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Economic Attribution Engine (EAE) converts operational correction into verified financial impact.

It ensures that every execution improvement is translated into measurable economic value.

Without this engine, execution improvements remain anecdotal.
With this engine, recovery becomes defensible at board level.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

Operational Signal → Structural Correction → Economic Translation → Attribution → Proof

No impact may be claimed without traceable mapping.

---

# 3. ECONOMIC IMPACT CATEGORIES

All improvements must map to one or more of the following:

1. Margin Expansion
2. Cost Reduction
3. Revenue Protection
4. Capacity Recovery
5. Working Capital Improvement
6. Risk Mitigation (Quantified)

If an improvement cannot be mapped, it cannot be reported.

---

# 4. ATTRIBUTION TIERS

## Tier 1 — Direct Impact
Clear before/after measurable delta.
Example: Downtime reduced 8% → Production increase → Revenue impact.

## Tier 2 — Indirect Impact
Secondary economic influence.
Example: SLA improvement → Customer retention → Revenue protection.

## Tier 3 — Systemic Impact
Structural long-term effect.
Example: Governance maturity → Reduced recurring leakage probability.

Each attribution must classify tier level.

---

# 5. ATTRIBUTION FORMULA STRUCTURE

Economic Impact = (Baseline – Post-Intervention Value) × Financial Multiplier

Where:
- Baseline = Pre-intervention measured state
- Post-Intervention = Verified new state
- Financial Multiplier = Revenue/unit margin/cost factor

All multipliers must be explicitly documented.

---

# 6. DOUBLE COUNTING PREVENTION

Rules:

1. No overlapping recovery claims across KPIs.
2. No compounding savings unless causal independence proven.
3. No forward projection without velocity validation.
4. All assumptions logged in Attribution Register.

---

# 7. RECOVERY VELOCITY MODEL

Impact must include:

- Time to detection
- Time to implementation
- Time to measurable impact

Recovery Velocity = Economic Impact ÷ Time to Realization

This prevents inflated projections.

---

# 8. RISK OF INACTION MODEL

For each major leakage:

Projected Loss = Current Leakage × 6–12 Month Continuity Assumption

Board view must include:
- Immediate recoverable
- Deferred recoverable
- Strategic risk exposure
