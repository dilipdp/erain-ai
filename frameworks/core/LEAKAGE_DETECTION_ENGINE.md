---

# 1. PURPOSE

The Leakage Detection Engine (LDE) identifies structural inefficiencies that erode economic value.

It converts raw operational signals into ranked leakage patterns.

Without detection, governance reacts late.
With detection, governance intervenes early.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

Signal → Deviation → Pattern → Structural Root Hypothesis → Economic Mapping → Priority Ranking

Detection must precede attribution.
Attribution must precede execution.

---

# 3. UNIVERSAL LEAKAGE TAXONOMY

All leakage must classify into one or more of the following:

1. Capacity Leakage
   - Downtime
   - Idle assets
   - Underutilization

2. Cost Leakage
   - Scrap / rework
   - Overtime inefficiency
   - Procurement variance

3. Revenue Leakage
   - SLA penalties
   - Lost sales
   - Delivery failures

4. Working Capital Leakage
   - Inventory excess
   - Receivable delays
   - Payable misalignment

5. Governance Leakage
   - Ownership ambiguity
   - Decision latency
   - Escalation avoidance

No new category may be added without Core review.

---

# 4. DETECTION MODES

## Mode A — Threshold Deviation

Trigger when KPI exceeds predefined deviation band.

Example:
- Downtime > baseline + tolerance
- SLA breach > X%

## Mode B — Pattern Recognition

Identify recurring anomalies across time, shift, site, or owner.

Example:
- Repeat breakdown by same machine
- Same vendor variance pattern

## Mode C — Structural Drift

Detect gradual degradation over time.

Example:
- OEE declining 1% monthly
- Increasing decision latency

## Mode D — Cross-Signal Correlation

Link multiple weak signals into structural pattern.

Example:
- High absenteeism + overtime spike + defect rise

---

# 5. BASELINE REQUIREMENTS

Every KPI must define:

- Historical baseline window
- Acceptable deviation band
- Seasonal adjustments (if required)
- Site-level variance tolerance

Baselines must be data-driven, not opinion-based.

---

# 6. ANOMALY RANKING LOGIC

Each detected leakage must receive:

Leakage Severity Score =
(Economic Exposure × Recurrence Frequency × Structural Risk Weight)

Where:
- Economic Exposure = Estimated monetary impact
- Recurrence Frequency = Occurrence density
- Structural Risk Weight = Governance vulnerability factor

Leakage list must be ranked descending by Severity Score.

---

# 7. FALSE POSITIVE CONTROL

To prevent noise inflation:

1. Require minimum signal confidence threshold.
2. Require cross-period validation for high severity.
3. Exclude single-event outliers unless economic impact is extreme.
4. Require human validation checkpoint for top 5 leakages.

---

# 8. ROOT HYPOTHESIS LINKAGE

Each leakage must generate:

- Probable structural causes (ranked)
- Governance exposure level
- Required validation steps

Detection does NOT equal root cause.
It generates ranked hypotheses.

---

# 9. ECONOMIC ENGINE INTEGRATION

Each detected leakage must include:

- Estimated economic exposure
- Attribution tier (Direct / Indirect / Systemic)
- Time-to-impact estimate
- Risk of inaction projection

No leakage may proceed to execution without economic mapping.

# 15. DETECTION TRIGGER CONDITIONS

The LDE activates Root Cause Intelligence when:

• Severity Score exceeds governance threshold
• Economic exposure crosses defined materiality level
• Leakage persists beyond two reporting cycles
• Governance leakage classified as High

If none of the above are met → leakage remains monitored but not escalated.

Escalation must be rule-based, not narrative-based.
