

# ERAIN AI™ — UNIVERSAL AUDIT QUESTION BANK  
Version: v1.0

---

## PURPOSE

This document defines the **standardized audit question architecture** used across all industries before collecting any client data.

Principles:
- No random questioning  
- No data dumping  
- No unnecessary document requests  
- Every question must map to: **Leakage → Root Cause → Impact → Owner → Cadence**

This structure works for:
- Manufacturing
- Logistics
- Services
- Solar / EPC
- Retail / Distribution
- Multi-site businesses
- Enterprises

---

# SECTION 1 — ORGANIZATION CONTEXT

### 1.1 Business Snapshot
- What do you produce / deliver?
- Revenue range (annual)?
- Number of sites / plants / branches?
- Number of employees?
- Key customer segments?

### 1.2 Strategic Focus
- Top 3 priorities this year?
- Biggest operational challenge?
- Where do you feel value is leaking?

### 1.3 Current Systems
- ERP / Tally / Excel / custom tools?
- Any BI dashboards?
- How often do you review performance?
- Who owns operational reporting?

---

# SECTION 2 — EXECUTION STRUCTURE

### 2.1 Ownership
- For each department, who is accountable?
- Are KPIs documented?
- Are targets clearly defined?
- Are owners reviewed weekly?

### 2.2 Cadence
- Do you run weekly review meetings?
- What is reviewed in those meetings?
- Are action items tracked?
- Is follow-up enforced?

### 2.3 Escalation
- When something goes wrong, what happens?
- Is there a documented escalation path?
- Are root causes documented?

---

# SECTION 3 — LEAKAGE DETECTION

This is the most important section.

## 3.1 Cost Leakage
- Where are costs increasing unexpectedly?
- Any recurring penalties?
- Rework / scrap / waste?
- Idle labor or equipment?

## 3.2 Time Leakage
- Project delays?
- Production downtime?
- Delivery SLA misses?
- Approval bottlenecks?

## 3.3 Revenue Leakage
- Discounting without control?
- Churn without root-cause?
- Stockouts?
- Unbilled work?

## 3.4 Decision Leakage
- Delayed decisions?
- Data inconsistency?
- Conflicting reports?
- No single source of truth?

---

# SECTION 4 — KPI STRUCTURE

### 4.1 KPI Visibility
- Do you have defined KPIs?
- Are they leading or lagging indicators?
- Are they reviewed weekly?

### 4.2 Financial KPIs
- Gross margin?
- Cost per unit?
- Cost to serve?
- Working capital cycle?

### 4.3 Operational KPIs
(Industry-specific, adaptable)
- OEE / uptime
- Throughput
- On-time delivery %
- SLA adherence
- Utilization %

---

# SECTION 5 — ROOT CAUSE CAPABILITY

- How do you perform root cause analysis?
- Do you use 5-Why / Fishbone?
- Is it documented?
- Are corrective actions tracked?
- Do issues repeat?

---

# SECTION 6 — DATA MATURITY

- Is data centralized?
- Is it real-time?
- Is it manually compiled?
- Are there conflicting versions?
- Who validates data accuracy?

---

# SECTION 7 — RISK & COMPLIANCE

- Any audit observations?
- Compliance penalties?
- Safety incidents?
- Vendor disputes?
- Contract slippages?

---

# SECTION 8 — IMPACT READINESS

- If we show quantified leakage, are you ready to act?
- Who signs off on change?
- Budget approval cycle?
- Change resistance level?

---

# SECTION 10 — DOCUMENT REQUEST MATRIX (ONLY AFTER DISCUSSION)

Do NOT request documents until:
- Initial call is completed
- Leakage hypothesis is formed
- Scope is defined

Potential documents:
- P&L (last 12 months)
- KPI reports
- Downtime logs
- Vendor spend summary
- SLA reports
- Project trackers

Always explain:
Why it’s needed  
What we will extract  
How it will be used
