# 21-Day Decision Governance Pilot

## Offer Identity
- Offer code: `21D-DG-PILOT`
- Motion: services-led pilot (scope-locked)
- Duration: 21 calendar days
- Geography: India-first, globally deployable

## Fixed Scope (No Drift)
1. Day 1-3: baseline leakage audit
2. Day 4-8: owner-mapped decision governance design
3. Day 9-14: ROI attribution model and recovery scenario pack
4. Day 15-18: executive readout and corrective-action sequencing
5. Day 19-21: board-ready pack (evidence + decision + expected value)

## Deliverables
- Ranked leakage ledger with owner mapping
- Decision governance map (trigger, owner, cadence, escalation)
- 30-60-90 execution roadmap with control points
- ROI attribution table linked to measurable operating KPIs
- Executive summary deck and implementation next-step brief

## Success Criteria
- P50 time-to-value trajectory <= 18 days
- Decision owner coverage >= 95%
- Reference-grade proof package generated at readout
