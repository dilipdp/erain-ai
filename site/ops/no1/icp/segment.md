# ICP Beachhead Segment

Primary segment (first 2 quarters):

- Industry: multi-site manufacturing and industrial services
- Company size: 100-1000 employees
- Geography: India + export-linked operators
- Decision makers: CEO, COO, CFO
- Trigger pain: cycle-time drift, quality leakage, delayed approvals, working-capital lock

Must-have fit signals:

1. Measurable value leakage in operations or decision cadence
2. Leadership willingness to assign owners and weekly governance cadence
3. Access to baseline data (ERP, Excel, Tally, process logs)

Do-not-target signals:

1. Looking for generic AI chatbot only
2. No owner accountability available
3. No execution bandwidth in next 30 days
