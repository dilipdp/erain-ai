# Customer Expansion 30/60/90 System

30-day review:

- Validate baseline and first intervention completion
- Confirm governance cadence and blockers

60-day review:

- Compare KPI movement vs baseline
- Publish owner-level execution scorecard

90-day review:

- Publish ROI attribution and payback progress
- Recommend expansion or scope correction

Expansion trigger:

- >= 2 KPI improvements with evidence-linked owner actions
