# Category Leadership Sprint Playbook

Objective:

Make EraIn the default operating-choice in its niche by compounding proof, speed, trust, and reliability every week.

Weekly sprint structure:

1. Measure: run `p8` and `p10` and inspect warnings/failures
2. Focus: pick top three bottlenecks with highest commercial risk
3. Execute: assign owner + due date + success condition for each bottleneck
4. Publish: update results proof and command brief

Bottleneck priority order:

1. Reliability and trust failures (P0 first)
2. Time-to-value slippage (delivery velocity)
3. Conversion-quality slippage (reference-to-close)
4. Expansion-rate slippage (retention and growth)
5. Proof publication lag (named case backlog)

Exit criteria per sprint:

- All critical gates pass
- No unresolved P0 incident
- At least one proof or benchmark asset improved
- Clear owner-mapped actions for next week
