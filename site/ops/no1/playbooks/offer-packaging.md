# Offer Packaging and Upgrade Triggers

Products:

1. Free Audit: diagnostic entry, no-card
2. Pro Audit: quantified leakage model + executive plan
3. OpsMind: recurring execution discipline and KPI control loops

Upgrade triggers:

- Free -> Pro:
  - leakage estimate >= INR 50L annualized
  - leadership commits sponsor and owner
- Pro -> OpsMind:
  - approved 30/60/90 roadmap
  - at least 3 KPIs require weekly governance

Pricing principle:

- Outcomes and verified value, not AI-hours.
