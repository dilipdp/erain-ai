1. Delivery lead (protect quality + speed)
2. Revenue operations owner (pipeline discipline)

- No hiring unless it removes a measured weekly bottleneck.

1. Where did deals stall this week?
2. Where did delivery miss SLA this week?
3. Where did reliability risk delay launch readiness?
