# Solar & EPC Project Execution Framework™

> Built for rooftop solar, C&I solar, EPC contractors, renewable developers, and multi-site project operators.

This framework is designed to eliminate schedule slip, margin leakage, vendor chaos, compliance delays, and execution ambiguity across solar and EPC environments.

It converts project complexity into a disciplined operating cadence:

**Truth → Decisions → Execution → Impact**

---

## 1. Where Solar & EPC Projects Leak Value

Across rooftop, C&I, and MW-scale projects, we consistently see:

### Commercial & Estimation
- Underpriced BOQ assumptions
- Incomplete site survey data
- Margin erosion from scope creep
- Weak cost-to-serve visibility

### Procurement & Vendor
- Module/inverter pricing volatility
- Delayed material arrival
- Vendor performance inconsistency
- Untracked variation claims

### Site Execution
- Idle labor
- Installation rework
- Structural mismatches
- Inverter/HT/LT coordination delays

### Compliance & Grid
- CEIG approval delays
- DISCOM documentation gaps
- Incomplete net-metering files
- Testing & commissioning surprises

### Financial & Cashflow
- Delayed billing milestones
- Retention money lockups
- Payment mismatch vs BOQ
- Project-level profitability blind spots
