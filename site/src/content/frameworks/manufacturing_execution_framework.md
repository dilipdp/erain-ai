# Manufacturing & Industrial Execution Framework

## Purpose
This framework defines how EraIn Audit AI™, Pro Audit™, OpsMind™, and Impact AI™ operate inside manufacturing and industrial environments.

Designed for:
- Multi-shift plants
- Discrete & process manufacturing
- Heavy industry & fabrication
- Multi-site industrial groups

This is an execution framework — not a dashboard template.

---

# 1. Core Failure Modes in Manufacturing

Across plants, value typically leaks through:

1. Hidden downtime (micro + unrecorded stops)
2. OEE distortion (data vs reality gap)
3. Scrap & rework not root-caused
4. Maintenance firefighting (not preventive)
5. Shift discipline breakdown
6. Vendor + spares leakage
7. Idle capacity masked by planning noise
8. Decision latency between plant and HO

EraIn installs structure around these failure modes.

---

# 2. EraIn Audit AI™ (Manufacturing Mode)

## Objective
Expose real operational leakage before recommending technology or automation.

## Inputs (Assumed Standard Set)
- Production logs
- Downtime sheets
- Maintenance logs
- Scrap/rework records
- Energy consumption data
- Vendor/spares purchase records
- Shift rosters
- Basic financial P&L (plant level)

## Outputs
- Ranked leakage map (₹ / $ impact)
- Downtime classification correction
- Scrap root-cause clustering
- Maintenance maturity score
- Capacity recovery estimate
- 30 / 60 / 90-day recovery roadmap

Deliverable: Executive-ready PDF + structured finding sheet.
