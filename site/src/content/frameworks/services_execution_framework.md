## 1. Purpose

This framework defines how EraIn audits, diagnoses, and upgrades service-based organizations where:

- Revenue depends on execution quality
- Margins depend on utilization + scope discipline
- Client trust depends on delivery predictability
- Leadership struggles with visibility across projects

This document ensures we never approach a services client in an ad-hoc way.

It standardizes:

- What we measure
- What we diagnose
- What we fix
- What proof we generate

---

## 2. Core Failure Patterns in Services Businesses

Across industries (IT services, marketing agencies, EPC services, consulting, facility management, SaaS delivery teams), we consistently see:

### A. Utilization Leakage
- Low billable utilization
- Hidden idle time
- Untracked effort
- Overstaffed projects

### B. Scope Creep & Margin Erosion
- Work beyond contract scope
- Underpriced change requests
- Poor time-to-billing discipline

### C. Delivery Chaos
- Missed milestones
- Poor owner accountability
- No clear escalation cadence
- Reactive firefighting

### D. Weak Profit Visibility
- No real project-level margin tracking
- Revenue recognized but effort misaligned
- Cost-to-serve unclear

### E. Leadership Blind Spots
- Too many dashboards, no clarity
- Decisions based on anecdotes
- No structured weekly execution cadence

EraIn addresses these structurally.

## 3. Audit Structure (Free Audit AI™ for Services)

### Step 1: Revenue & Delivery Snapshot

We assess:

- Total active projects
- Average contract value
- Billing model (fixed / T&M / hybrid)
- Delivery teams & structure
- Current utilization %

Output:
- Service health baseline

We evaluate:

- Billable vs non-billable ratio
- Time-tracking discipline
- Capacity vs demand mismatch
- Project staffing patterns

- Hidden capacity recovery potential
- Margin improvement estimate

### Step 3: Scope & Margin Discipline

We diagnose:

- Change request governance
- Scope documentation clarity
- Pricing integrity
- Delivery vs contracted effort

Output:
- Margin leakage mapping
- Recovery strategy

### Step 4: Execution Cadence Mapping

We analyze:

- Weekly review structure
- Escalation process
- Owner clarity
- Decision latency

Output:
- Execution gap heatmap
