# EraIn Logistics & Supply Chain Execution Framework™

## Purpose

This framework is designed for logistics companies, transport operators, warehousing networks, 3PLs, distribution businesses, and supply-chain-heavy enterprises.

It identifies:
- Delay risks
- Cost-to-serve leakage
- Penalty exposure
- Route inefficiencies
- Vendor / fleet performance gaps
- Inventory placement errors
- SLA failures
- Accountability breakdowns

This framework is data-ready and can be deployed immediately once client data is collected.

---

# Phase 1 — Truth Mapping (Operational Reality Audit)

## 1. Route & Throughput Reality

We map:

- Average planned vs actual delivery time
- Route-level delay variance
- On-time delivery %
- Repeat-delay corridors
- High-penalty routes

Outputs:
- Ranked delay corridors
- SLA risk map
- Bottleneck identification
- Delay root-cause clusters

---

## 2. Cost-to-Serve Breakdown

We decompose:

- Fuel per route
- Driver overtime
- Idle time
- Detention costs
- Warehouse dwell time
- Reverse logistics burden
- Penalties and claim leakage

Outputs:
- True cost-per-route
- Cost-per-customer
- Cost-per-corridor
- Hidden loss map

## 3. Fleet & Asset Utilization

We measure:

- Vehicle utilization %

- Repeat breakdown patterns

- Underutilized asset list
- Idle capital exposure
