Across industries, the failure patterns are predictable:

## Operational Leakage
- Unplanned downtime

- OEE misinterpretation

- Idle labor during shift overlap

## Financial Leakage
- Margin erosion not tied to root cause
- Energy cost inefficiencies
- Vendor pricing creep
- Inventory carrying drag
- Working capital blockage

## Execution Breakdown
- Weak shift handover discipline
- No owner per bottleneck
- CAPA without accountability
- Delayed escalation loops
- Data exists but not action-linked

EraIn installs a loop that eliminates these patterns.
