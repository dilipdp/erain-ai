# 📦 EraIn Logistics & Supply Chain Execution Framework™

## Executive Positioning

Logistics failures rarely explode. They erode margin silently through compounding micro-inefficiencies across transport, warehousing, inventory, and vendor coordination.

Most organizations cannot answer with precision:
- What is the true cost per route?
- Where is working capital stuck?
- Which vendors are structurally underperforming?
- How much margin is lost due to coordination gaps?

EraIn installs a quantified operating discipline across transport, warehousing, inventory, and multi-node network orchestration — tying every inefficiency to measurable financial impact.

---

# 1️⃣ Transport & Fleet Performance Model

## Core Metrics

- Cost per km / mile
- Cost per delivery
- Fleet utilization %
- Idle time ratio
- On-time dispatch %
- On-time delivery %
- Detention hours
- Fuel variance per vehicle
- Route deviation frequency

## Audit Questions

- Are routes optimized or historically repeated?
- How many vehicles operate below 65% utilization?
- Is detention tracked at customer level?
- Are penalty clauses monitored in real time?
- Is fuel variance analyzed by driver?

## Financial Impact Model

Hidden Loss Areas:

- Idle fleet depreciation
- Overtime labor
- Fuel inefficiency
- SLA penalties
- Under-billed deliveries
- Re-dispatch cost

EraIn calculates:

True Cost per Route =  
Fixed Cost + Variable Cost + Delay Cost + Penalty Risk + Opportunity Cost

# 2️⃣ Warehouse & Throughput Model

## Core Metrics

- Dock-to-stock time
- Pick accuracy %
- Order cycle time
- Inventory accuracy %
- Shrinkage %
- Throughput per labor hour
- Stock-out frequency
- Return processing time

## Failure Modes

- Manual picking errors
- Poor slotting strategy
- Slow reconciliation cycles
- Weak inbound scheduling
- Misaligned reorder points

## Financial Impact Model

Cost of Inefficiency =  
Rework + Returns + Idle Labor + Lost Sales + Emergency Freight

# 3️⃣ Inventory & Working Capital Model

## Metrics

- Inventory turnover
- Days inventory outstanding (DIO)
- Dead stock %
- Obsolete inventory %
- Service level %
- Fill rate %

## Risk Areas

- Overstocking to compensate for poor forecasting
- Understocking due to reactive planning
- Capital trapped in slow-moving SKUs
- Demand variability misinterpretation

EraIn measures:

Working Capital Velocity  
= Revenue / Average Inventory Value

Capital Release Potential  
= Excess Inventory × Cost of Capital %
