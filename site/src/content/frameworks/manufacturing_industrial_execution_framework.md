## 3. Diagnostic Architecture

### Layer 1: Reality Audit

We verify:

- OEE (Availability × Performance × Quality)
- Downtime patterns (planned vs unplanned)
- Scrap rate
- Rework frequency
- Maintenance backlog
- Vendor reliability
- Procurement exceptions
- Energy consumption anomalies
- Idle labor time
- Shift-level productivity variance

Output:
- Ranked leakage map
- ₹/$ impact estimation
- Ownership gap identification

---

### Layer 2: Root-Cause Mapping

We classify leaks into:

1. Process failure
2. Equipment reliability issue
3. Vendor / supply disruption
4. Shift discipline breakdown
5. Maintenance cadence failure
6. Decision bottleneck
7. Approval chain delay
8. Skill / training gap

Each issue gets:

- Financial impact
- Responsible owner
- Recovery path
- 30/60/90 plan

---

### Layer 3: Execution Installation

EraIn installs:

- Shift-level accountability grid
- Weekly plant review cadence
- Maintenance discipline tracker
- Spare governance controls
- Vendor performance index
- Downtime early-warning alerts
- Incident root-cause log
- Escalation workflow

No dashboards without ownership.
