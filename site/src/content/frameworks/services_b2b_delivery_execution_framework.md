## 1. Why Services Businesses Leak Value

Across B2B services, consulting, agencies, IT delivery, field services, and managed services, the same failure patterns repeat:

- Revenue booked but margin unknown  
- Utilization reported but not trusted  
- Projects "almost profitable" but not measured weekly  
- Rework cycles hidden inside delivery teams  
- Client churn blamed on market, not execution  
- Founders overloaded with approvals and escalations

Services don’t usually fail from lack of sales.  
They fail from weak execution discipline and invisible margin erosion.

This framework installs:

**Truth → Ownership → Cadence → Margin Proof**

## 2. Core Diagnostic Areas (Audit Layer)

### A. Revenue Quality
- Revenue by client
- Revenue by service line
- Revenue by team / delivery pod
- Recurring vs one-time
- Discounting patterns

### B. Margin Reality
- Gross margin per client
- Effective hourly realization
- Write-offs and scope creep
- Hidden unpaid effort
- Rework hours

### C. Utilization Discipline
- Billable vs non-billable hours
- Bench strength
- Overloaded high performers
- Under-utilized team members

### D. Delivery Health
- SLA compliance
- Deadline slippage patterns
- Escalation frequency
- Client complaint categories
- Change request frequency

### E. Cash & Contract Control
- Invoicing lag
- Payment cycles
- Credit terms mismatch
- Contract leakage
- Renewal discipline

## 4. Common Root Causes (What We Usually Find)

- Pricing misaligned with effort reality
- Weak scope control
- No owner per client profitability
- Founder as escalation hub
- Delivery leaders not measured on margin
- No weekly margin review
- Utilization reported monthly (too late)

The problem is rarely talent.
It is usually system design.
