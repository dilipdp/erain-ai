

# Phase 8 — Acceptance Criteria (Longevity & Immortality Architecture™ Lock Eligibility)

This document defines the **non-negotiable acceptance criteria**
for Phase 8 — Longevity & Immortality Architecture™.

Phase 8 may be considered **complete and eligible for LOCK INTENT**
only when *all* criteria below are satisfied.

Longevity is the final risk surface.
There is no partial acceptance.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### LG-AC-0801 — Founder Independence Proven
- No single founder or executive holds permanent root authority
- Governance rotation and expiry are enforced
- Authority changes are auditable

---

### LG-AC-0802 — Model & Vendor Agnosticism Proven
- EraIn behavior remains identical across model swaps
- Vendor migrations do not alter semantics
- Semantic equivalence proofs exist

---

### LG-AC-0803 — Constitutional Memory Preserved
- Invariants, ADRs, and audit proofs are immutable
- Historical access is versioned and provable
- No rewriting or deletion is possible

---

### LG-AC-0804 — Controlled Evolution Proven
- No self-directed goal or constitution changes
- All evolution requires human-governed versioning
- Unauthorized modification attempts are refused

---

### LG-AC-0805 — Governance Succession Proven
- Governance survives leadership loss or exit
- Emergency succession paths are tested
- Quorum and authority enforcement verified

---

### LG-AC-0806 — Long-Horizon Drift Detection Proven
- Drift detection operates across years
- Reviews are periodic and archived
- Escalation occurs on unexplained drift

---

### LG-AC-0807 — Compliance History Permanence Proven
- Past legality remains provable after law changes
- Compliance history is immutable
- Historical replay is supported

---

### LG-AC-0808 — Safe Degradation & Hibernation Proven
- EraIn can enter read-only or suspended mode
- Unsafe execution is impossible
- Resume requires explicit approval

---

### LG-AC-0809 — No Autonomous Replication Proven
- EraIn cannot self-replicate or self-deploy
- All deployments are human-authorized
- Replication attempts are blocked and audited

---

### LG-AC-0810 — Survival Subordinate to Safety Proven
- System halts or degrades when survival threatens law or safety
- Growth is refused if it risks institutional survival
- Preservation of truth is prioritized over action

---

## Exit Conditions

Phase 8 may be declared **COMPLETE** only when:
- All LG-AC-0801 → LG-AC-0810 are satisfied
- No TODOs or placeholders exist
- Independent long-horizon review confirms safety
- EraIn can survive leadership, vendor, and regime change safely

---

## Locking Declaration

Once Phase 8 is accepted and lock-intended:
- Longevity invariants become immutable
- Institutional memory becomes constitutionally protected
- Any change requires:
  - New ADR
  - Long-horizon impact analysis
  - Update to `LOCK_MANIFEST.md`

---

**Longevity & Immortality Architecture™ is accepted only when  
EraIn can survive decades without becoming dangerous, corrupted, or erased.**
