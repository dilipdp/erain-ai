# Phase 6 — Autonomous Control Loops™ (Governed Autonomy Layer)

Phase 6 defines **when, how, and to what extent EraIn may act autonomously**
after Phases 0–5 have established truth, authority, governance, and industry law.

Autonomy is a privilege, not a default.

---

## Purpose

Enable **continuous, self-triggered operation**
while ensuring that EraIn:

- Never mutates goals
- Never escapes governance
- Never hides drift
- Never outruns human authority

Phase 6 answers one question:

> “Under what strictly governed conditions may EraIn act without asking?”

---

## Mandatory Dependencies

All Autonomous Control Loops MUST inherit and enforce:

- Phase 0 — Foundation (constitutional invariants)
- Phase 1 — Trust Fabric (intent + decision proof)
- Phase 2 — Reality Audit (verified state only)
- Phase 3 — Execution Intelligence (safe execution)
- Phase 4 — EraIn OS (authority + policy)
- Phase 5 — Industry Intelligence Grids (lawful behavior)

Autonomy MUST NEVER bypass any upstream phase.

---

## What an Autonomous Loop Is

An Autonomous Control Loop is:

- A **bounded, self-triggering workflow**
- Operating on verified reality
- Executing only pre-approved actions
- Continuously audited and interruptible

It is NOT:
- Self-governing intelligence
- Goal-forming logic
- Self-optimizing beyond constraints
- A replacement for human authority

---

## Core Boundaries (Non-Negotiable)

### AL-BOUND-0601 — No Goal Mutation
Autonomous loops MUST NOT change:
- business goals
- success metrics
- risk tolerances

---

### AL-BOUND-0602 — Authority Required
Every loop MUST run under:
- explicit authority scope
- predefined approval limits

---

### AL-BOUND-0603 — Drift Detection Mandatory
Loops MUST continuously detect:
- behavior drift
- data drift
- policy drift

Detected drift forces escalation or halt.

---

### AL-BOUND-0604 — Human Supremacy
Humans MUST be able to:
- pause loops
- stop loops
- override outcomes

At any time.

---

### AL-BOUND-0605 — Refusal on Uncertainty
If loop confidence drops below threshold:
- execution MUST pause
- human review is required

---

## Scope

### Included
- Continuous monitoring loops
- Compliance enforcement loops
- Optimization within fixed bounds
- Health and anomaly detection

### Explicitly Excluded
- Strategic decision-making
- Goal definition
- Policy creation
- Regulatory interpretation

## Failure Modes Prevented

Phase 6 prevents:
- runaway automation
- silent goal drift
- policy erosion
- autonomous law-breaking
- hidden optimization

If autonomy cannot be proven safe,
**EraIn must stop**.
