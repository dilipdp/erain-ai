# Phase 4 — EraIn OS™ (Sovereign Business Operating System)

Phase 4 defines **EraIn as a complete business operating system**, not a tool, SaaS, or assistant.
It is the layer that turns intelligence, truth, and execution into a **coherent, governable enterprise reality**.

EraIn OS™ sits above all prior phases and enforces them at runtime.

---

## Purpose

Replace fragmented SaaS, dashboards, and workflows with a **single intelligence spine**
that runs the business end-to-end — with proof, truth, and control built in.

EraIn OS answers one question:

> “How does a real business operate when intelligence is native?”

---

## Mandatory Dependencies

Phase 4 MAY operate only if it enforces:

### From Phase 0 — Foundation
- Constitutional invariants
- Phase locking rules
- Longevity-first design

### From Phase 1 — Trust Fabric
- Proven decisions only
- Full decision provenance
- Refusal semantics

### From Phase 2 — Reality Audit
- Verified State Snapshots only
- Reality Confidence Index propagation
- Contradiction visibility

### From Phase 3 — Execution Intelligence
- Safe execution only
- Rollback / containment enforcement
- Kill-switch supremacy

EraIn OS MUST NEVER bypass lower phases.
It exists to **orchestrate**, not override.

---

## Scope

### Included
- Business-domain orchestration
- Cross-domain state management
- Role-based access & authority
- Policy enforcement
- Audit-native workflows
- Human-in-the-loop control
- Executive oversight surfaces

### Explicitly Excluded
- Core decision intelligence
- Reality verification
- Direct execution logic
- Model-level reasoning

EraIn OS is the **governor**, not the brain.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Domain Capsules
Each business domain runs as a **capsule** with:
- Clear inputs (verified state)
- Clear outputs (auditable actions)
- Defined authority boundaries

Example domains:
- Finance
- Operations
- Compliance
- Growth
- Procurement
- HR
- Executive Control

---

### 2. Policy & Authority Engine
- Defines who can do what, when, and why
- Enforces separation of duties
- Supports escalation, approval, and override flows

All authority is:
- Explicit
- Auditable
- Revocable

---

### 3. Cross-Domain State Graph
- Single source of business truth
- Built from Verified State Snapshots
- Prevents domain-level contradictions

---

### 4. Workflow Orchestration Layer
- Turns decisions into governed workflows
- Injects human approvals where required
- Refuses workflows that violate policy or confidence thresholds

---

### 5. Executive Control Plane
- Real-time visibility into:
  - What the business believes
  - What it is doing
  - Why it is acting
- Kill-switches at business and system level
- Scenario simulation and impact preview

---

## Failure Modes Addressed

Phase 4 prevents:

- Tool sprawl
- Shadow workflows
- Invisible authority
- Unapproved execution
- Cross-domain contradictions
- “Dashboard theater” without control

If a workflow cannot be governed, **it must not exist**.

## Acceptance Criteria (Preview)

Phase 4 may be considered complete only when:
- All business domains operate via EraIn OS
- No domain bypasses Trust, Reality, or Execution layers
- Authority and policy enforcement is deterministic
- Executives can halt, inspect, or override workflows
- All actions remain provable and reversible

Formal criteria will be defined in `specs/acceptance.md`.

## Locking Intent

Phase 4 will be lockable once:
- OS invariants are explicit
- Policy engine is complete
- Cross-domain enforcement is proven
- Phase 0–3 compliance is guaranteed at runtime

Once locked, EraIn OS semantics become non-negotiable.
