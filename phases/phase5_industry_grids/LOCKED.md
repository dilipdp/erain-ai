## Allowed Changes After Lock

- Clarifying documentation
- Additive regulations or constraints
- New risk models
- Versioned grid upgrades
- New ADRs that **extend**, not weaken, compliance

---

## Forbidden Changes After Lock

- Weakening industry compliance rules
- Allowing industry actions without regulation references
- Guessing or approximating law
- Silent rule changes
- Cross-industry rule reuse without mediation
