

# Phase 5 — Industry Intelligence Grids Invariants (Vertical Sovereignty Laws)

These invariants define the **non-negotiable rules** that apply to *all* Industry Intelligence Grids™.
They ensure that vertical specialization **never weakens** EraIn’s constitutional, governance,
safety, or audit guarantees.

All Industry Grids are constrained by Phases 0–4.
If any invariant below is violated, the industry grid is invalid by definition.

---

## IG-INV-0501 — No Governance Weakening

**Rule**  
An Industry Grid MUST NOT weaken, bypass, or override any invariant
from Phases 0–4.

Industry-specific logic may only **add constraints**, never remove them.

**Prevents**
- “Industry exception” loopholes
- Regulatory shortcuts

**Validation**
- CI checks inheritance of all upstream invariants
- Refusal on missing or overridden constraints

---

## IG-INV-0502 — Industry Law Is Explicit

**Rule**  
All industry rules MUST be:
- Explicitly documented
- Machine-enforceable
- Auditable

Implicit or assumed industry behavior is forbidden.

**Prevents**
- Tribal knowledge errors
- Hidden compliance gaps

**Validation**
- All actions reference explicit industry rules
- Missing rules cause refusal

---

## IG-INV-0503 — Compliance Before Optimization

**Rule**  
Industry compliance, safety, and legality ALWAYS override:
- Efficiency
- Cost
- Speed
- Optimization goals

**Prevents**
- Profit-driven violations
- Unsafe shortcuts

**Validation**
- Compliance checks run before execution
- Violations block action

---

## IG-INV-0504 — Industry-Specific Reality Constraints

**Rule**  
Reality Audit for an industry MUST include:
- Technical constraints
- Legal constraints
- Environmental constraints
- Economic feasibility constraints

Generic reality models are insufficient.

**Prevents**
- Acting on industry-incorrect assumptions

**Validation**
- Snapshot includes industry constraint proofs
- Missing constraints downgrade or refuse

---

## IG-INV-0505 — Risk & Failure Models Are Mandatory

**Rule**  
Each Industry Grid MUST define:
- Common failure modes
- Catastrophic failure modes
- Regulatory failure scenarios

Unknown risks MUST cause refusal.

**Prevents**
- Blind operation in high-risk domains

**Validation**
- Execution checks failure model coverage
- Unmodeled risk blocks action

---

## IG-INV-0506 — Industry Isolation

**Rule**  
An Industry Grid MUST NOT:
- Leak rules into other industries
- Reuse constraints blindly across industries

Cross-industry reuse MUST be explicit and mediated by EraIn OS.

**Prevents**
- Cross-industry contamination
- Incorrect rule transfer

**Validation**
- Industry identifiers enforced at runtime
- Cross-industry calls require mediation

---

## IG-INV-0507 — Industry Audit Completeness

**Rule**  
All industry actions MUST emit:
- Industry identifier
- Applicable regulation references
- Compliance evaluation results

**Prevents**
- Unprovable regulatory compliance

**Validation**
- Missing industry audit fields cause hard failure

---

## IG-INV-0508 — Human Escalation Is Mandatory

**Rule**  
Industry actions involving:
- Safety risk
- Legal ambiguity
- Regulatory interpretation

MUST escalate to human review.

**Prevents**
- Autonomous misinterpretation of law

**Validation**
- Escalation paths enforced
- Non-escalated ambiguous actions refused

---

## IG-INV-0509 — Industry Evolution Is Versioned

**Rule**  
Industry rules MUST evolve via:
- Versioned grids
- ADR-backed changes
- Backward compatibility where possible

Silent rule changes are forbidden.

**Prevents**
- Regulatory drift
- Undetected behavior changes

**Validation**
- Version tags required
- Rule diffs auditable

---

## IG-INV-0510 — Refusal Is the Default on Uncertainty

**Rule**  
If industry law, constraint, or risk is uncertain or unknown,
EraIn MUST refuse to act.

**Prevents**
- Guesswork in regulated domains

**Validation**
- Uncertainty flags propagate
- Refusal paths tested

---

## Enforcement Summary

- All invariants are mandatory
- Industry Grids inherit Phases 0–4 automatically
- Violations block execution
- No exceptions, no silent degradation
