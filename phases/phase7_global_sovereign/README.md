# Phase 7 — Global Sovereign Mode™ (Jurisdiction & Civilizational Layer)

Phase 7 defines how EraIn operates **across countries, jurisdictions, and sovereign regimes**
without violating law, culture, or political authority.

This phase ensures EraIn is not just compliant —
but **sovereign-aware, jurisdiction-respecting, and globally survivable**.

---

## Purpose

Enable EraIn to function across:
- multiple countries
- conflicting legal systems
- divergent regulatory philosophies
- geopolitical boundaries

Phase 7 answers one question:

> “How does EraIn behave correctly when the world itself disagrees?”

---

## Mandatory Dependencies

Global Sovereign Mode™ MUST inherit and enforce:

- Phase 0 — Foundation (constitutional invariants)
- Phase 1 — Trust Fabric (proven intent)
- Phase 2 — Reality Audit (verified state)
- Phase 3 — Execution Intelligence (safe action)
- Phase 4 — EraIn OS (governance & authority)
- Phase 5 — Industry Intelligence Grids (sector law)
- Phase 6 — Autonomous Control Loops (bounded autonomy)

Sovereignty is layered **above**, never instead of, governance.

---

## What Sovereign Mode Is

Sovereign Mode is:

- A **jurisdictional intelligence layer**
- A **law-conflict resolution engine**
- A **deployment & control boundary**
- A **civilizational compatibility layer**

It is NOT:
- A single “global policy”
- A bypass for national law
- A centralized super-authority

EraIn adapts to sovereignty — it does not erase it.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Jurisdiction Engine
- Models country-specific laws
- Tracks regulatory updates
- Enforces local overrides where required
- Prevents illegal cross-border execution

---

### 2. Conflict-of-Law Resolution
- Detects contradictory legal requirements
- Chooses safe refusal or escalation
- Surfaces conflicts to humans and regulators

If laws conflict and cannot be reconciled,
**EraIn must refuse to act**.

---

### 3. Sovereign Deployment Modes
EraIn may operate as:
- Fully sovereign (nation-hosted)
- Hybrid (local + global coordination)
- Isolated (air-gapped, country-only)
- Observer-only (read, no act)

Mode selection is explicit and auditable.

---

### 4. Data Residency & Control
- Enforces data localization
- Prevents unlawful data movement
- Supports jurisdiction-specific retention and deletion

---

### 5. Sovereign Override Channels
- Lawful government orders
- Court-mandated actions
- Emergency national controls

Overrides are:
- explicit
- auditable
- constrained by Phase 0 human rights invariants

---

## Scope

### Included
- Jurisdiction modeling
- Cross-border execution controls
- Data residency enforcement
- Sovereign audit trails
- Regulatory coexistence

### Explicitly Excluded
- Political decision-making
- Value imposition
- Policy advocacy

EraIn obeys law — it does not set it.

---

## Failure Modes Prevented

Phase 7 prevents:
- illegal cross-border actions
- data sovereignty violations
- one-country rules leaking into another
- “global AI” ignoring local law
- silent compliance drift

If sovereignty cannot be respected,
**EraIn must stop**.
