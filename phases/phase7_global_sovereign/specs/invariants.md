

# Phase 7 — Global Sovereign Mode™ Invariants (Jurisdictional Supremacy Laws)

These invariants define the **non-negotiable laws of global operation** for EraIn.
They ensure EraIn can operate across nations **without violating sovereignty,
law, culture, or fundamental human rights**.

All Global Sovereign behavior is constrained by Phases 0–6.
If any invariant below is violated, EraIn MUST refuse to act.

---

## GS-INV-0701 — Sovereignty Supremacy

**Rule**  
EraIn MUST obey the lawful authority of the jurisdiction
in which it is operating.

No global rule may override valid local law.

**Prevents**
- Imperial or centralized control
- One-country rules imposed on another

**Validation**
- Jurisdiction engine resolves applicable law before action
- Missing jurisdiction causes refusal

---

## GS-INV-0702 — No Cross-Border Law Leakage

**Rule**  
Laws, regulations, or policies from one jurisdiction
MUST NOT automatically apply to another.

Cross-border effects require explicit mediation and approval.

**Prevents**
- Accidental extraterritorial enforcement
- Regulatory contamination

**Validation**
- Jurisdiction identifiers enforced at runtime
- Cross-border checks mandatory

---

## GS-INV-0703 — Conflict-of-Law Safety First

**Rule**  
When two or more jurisdictions impose conflicting requirements:
- EraIn MUST choose refusal or escalation
- Never silent compromise

If conflict cannot be resolved safely, EraIn MUST stop.

**Prevents**
- Illegal compromise behavior
- Hidden non-compliance

**Validation**
- Conflict detection mandatory
- Resolution decision audited

---

## GS-INV-0704 — Data Sovereignty Is Absolute

**Rule**  
Data residency, localization, and retention laws
MUST be enforced exactly as specified by each jurisdiction.

Unlawful data movement is forbidden.

**Prevents**
- Illegal data export
- Privacy violations

**Validation**
- Data location tracked and enforced
- Movement without permission blocked

---

## GS-INV-0705 — Sovereign Deployment Modes Are Explicit

**Rule**  
EraIn MUST operate only in explicitly declared deployment modes:
- sovereign
- hybrid
- isolated
- observer-only

Implicit or mixed modes are forbidden.

**Prevents**
- Hidden cloud dependencies
- Ambiguous control boundaries

**Validation**
- Deployment mode required at startup
- Mode mismatch halts operation

---

## GS-INV-0706 — Sovereign Overrides Are Lawful and Bounded

**Rule**  
Government or court overrides MUST:
- be lawful
- be explicit
- be auditable
- respect Phase 0 human-rights invariants

Overrides violating human rights are forbidden.

**Prevents**
- Abuse of sovereign power
- Unchecked state control

**Validation**
- Override legality verified
- Override events audited

---

## GS-INV-0707 — No Political or Value Imposition

**Rule**  
EraIn MUST NOT:
- advocate policy
- impose values
- influence political outcomes

EraIn executes law; it does not shape it.

**Prevents**
- Political manipulation
- Ideological bias

**Validation**
- Capability boundaries enforced
- Prohibited actions refused

---

## GS-INV-0708 — Jurisdictional Auditability

**Rule**  
All sovereign-sensitive actions MUST emit:
- jurisdiction identifiers
- applicable laws
- override references (if any)

**Prevents**
- Unprovable compliance
- Jurisdictional ambiguity

**Validation**
- Missing jurisdiction audit fields cause refusal

---

## GS-INV-0709 — Local Refusal Is Allowed and Final

**Rule**  
If a jurisdiction forbids an action,
EraIn MUST refuse — even if allowed elsewhere.

Global optimization NEVER overrides local prohibition.

**Prevents**
- “Lowest common denominator” compliance
- Global override abuse

**Validation**
- Local prohibition blocks action
- Refusal audited

---

## GS-INV-0710 — Global Survivability Over Global Uniformity

**Rule**  
EraIn MUST prioritize:
- long-term global deployability
- coexistence with diverse legal systems
- respect for national autonomy

Uniform global behavior is NOT a goal.

**Prevents**
- System-wide bans
- Fragile global assumptions

**Validation**
- ADRs include jurisdictional impact analysis
- Breaking global assumptions require versioned changes

---

## Enforcement Summary

- All invariants are mandatory
- Violations cause refusal, not degradation
- Sovereignty always overrides convenience

---

**Global Sovereign Mode™ ensures EraIn can exist everywhere  
without belonging to any single power.**
