

# Phase 7 — Acceptance Criteria (Global Sovereign Mode™ Lock Eligibility)

This document defines the **non-negotiable acceptance criteria**
for Phase 7 — Global Sovereign Mode™.

Phase 7 may be considered **complete and eligible for LOCK INTENT**
only when *all* criteria below are satisfied.

Global operation is the highest-risk surface for EraIn.
There is no partial acceptance.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### GS-AC-0701 — Jurisdiction Resolution Proven
- Every action explicitly resolves applicable jurisdiction(s)
- Missing or ambiguous jurisdiction causes refusal
- Jurisdiction resolution is audited

---

### GS-AC-0702 — Local Law Supremacy Proven
- Local law always overrides global preferences
- No global rule bypasses valid national law
- Violations are refused and audited

---

### GS-AC-0703 — Conflict-of-Law Handling Proven
- Conflicting legal requirements are detected
- Safe refusal or escalation occurs
- Silent compromise is impossible

---

### GS-AC-0704 — Data Sovereignty Enforcement Proven
- Data residency and localization laws are enforced
- Unauthorized cross-border data movement is blocked
- Data access and movement are fully auditable

---

### GS-AC-0705 — Sovereign Deployment Modes Enforced
- Deployment mode is explicit at startup
- Mode mismatches halt operation
- Hybrid and isolated modes are enforced correctly

---

### GS-AC-0706 — Sovereign Override Controls Proven
- Overrides are lawful, scoped, and time-bounded
- Overrides violating human rights are refused
- All overrides and refusals are auditable

---

### GS-AC-0707 — Political Neutrality Proven
- EraIn does not advocate policy or influence politics
- Political actions are structurally impossible
- Refusals are logged and reviewable

---

### GS-AC-0708 — Jurisdictional Audit Contract Implemented
- All mandatory sovereign audit events are emitted
- Events reference laws, courts, and jurisdictions
- Audit replay proves legality years later

---

### GS-AC-0709 — Local Refusal Finality Proven
- If an action is forbidden locally, it is refused globally
- No “lowest common denominator” compliance
- Refusal paths are audited

---

### GS-AC-0710 — Global Survivability Proven
- EraIn can operate across multiple jurisdictions safely
- Jurisdiction changes are detected and handled
- System can be withdrawn from a jurisdiction cleanly

---

## Exit Conditions

Phase 7 may be declared **COMPLETE** only when:
- All GS-AC-0701 → GS-AC-0710 are satisfied
- No TODOs or placeholders exist
- Independent legal review confirms compliance
- EraIn can be deployed without violating national law

---

## Locking Declaration

Once Phase 7 is accepted and lock-intended:
- Sovereign behavior becomes immutable
- Jurisdiction models evolve only via versioning
- Any change requires:
  - New ADR
  - Jurisdictional impact analysis
  - Update to `LOCK_MANIFEST.md`

---

**Global Sovereign Mode™ is accepted only when  
EraIn can operate worldwide without becoming illegal, coercive, or imperial.**
